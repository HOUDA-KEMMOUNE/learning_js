    ------------------
    |     username   |

using destructuring.

    players[0].address.city

I want you to practice destructuring.

        player.username

Part 7 — The final challenge 🔥

Create:
    const getOnlineUsernames = ...

It should return:
    ["Houda", "Sara"]

    players
